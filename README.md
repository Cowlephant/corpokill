# corpokill
Destroy those corporations who are destroying us with forced AI adoption, using legal boycotting.

# About

The rise of AI, specifically generative AI and its derivatives is having a profound impact on the world. Every individual has their own opinions on the value of AI in their life, both personally and professionally, and how they feel about the ethics of the origin and expansion of these technologies. The creators of this project have their own nuanced opinions on AI.

What this project is, is not a blanketed rejection of AI, but a rejection of the corporations of the world forcing this AI upon its employees, consumers and general population as a whole.

Whether one chooses to use AI in their personal or professional life should be exactly that, a choice, and not a mandate from corporations or society at large. The usage of AI tools like Bossware to track and police their employees is unacceptable. The "adopt-or-die" mandate by companies for employees to use AI tools to replace their earned skills and capabilities is unacceptable.

We as the people built these companies up through our support, and we can certainly tear them down through our coordinated boycott. They have been imposing their will upon us and deciding how we will embrace their dystopian future, and now it is our turn to demonstrate to them that we in fact are the ones who choose and in fact build the future we want.
